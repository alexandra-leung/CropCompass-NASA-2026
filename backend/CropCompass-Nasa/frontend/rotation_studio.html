See formulas and model boundaries

Crop evapotranspiration: ETc = Kc × ET₀. Per-crop approximate net irrigation: max(0, ETc − assumed effective rainfall). Gross irrigation depth: net irrigation / assumed efficiency. Volume: depth in mm × field area in ha × 10 m³/(mm·ha). Water-feasible means modeled demand ≤ the specified water budget for the entire crop sequence.

Three plans occupy different total growing durations. No yield, profitability, actual soil nutrient gain, crop-specific climate threshold, or field-level suitability is predicted. Climate projections do not presently drive crop-water calculations.
